Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Short version: we collect your email, a hashed password, and whatever plan you pick. Paid plans route billing details to Stripe — we never see or store your card number. We don't sell your data. This is a plain-language starting point, not a substitute for a compliance review before you scale beyond a pilot.

1. Data we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account dataName, email, hashed passwordCreate and secure your account
Plan & billingSelected tier, subscription statusDetermine what features you can access; billing details themselves are held by Stripe, not us
Saved searchesFilters you save for alertsSend you matching-parcel notifications
Usage & audit logsLogin times, admin actions, exportsSecurity, abuse prevention, and — for county staff — an accountable audit trail
Technical dataIP address (rate limiting only), browser infoPrevent abuse, keep the site running

2. Data we publish

Parcel listings (owner names, addresses, amounts owed, legal descriptions) come from county tax offices and are already public record before we touch them. Publishing this data is the product itself — it is not personal data we collected from you.

3. Cookies

We use two functional cookies, both httpOnly (invisible to page scripts): one for your investor account session, one for county-staff back-office sessions. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

4. Third parties we share data with

ProviderWhat they receivePurpose
StripeEmail, payment detailsSubscription billing
Email provider (SendGrid or Resend)Email address, message contentAlerts, password resets, receipts
Hosting/database providerAll account and listing dataRunning the service

We do not sell personal data to data brokers or advertisers.

5. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure for legal and audit purposes. Audit-log entries (who published or changed what) are retained indefinitely for county accountability and are never deleted, only superseded by later entries.

6. Your choices

You can update your account details, unsubscribe from alert emails, or close your account at any time by contacting help@landrollleader.com. Because parcel data reflects public county records, we can't remove a public listing at an individual's request — that request should go to the county that published the underlying record.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text); sessions use httpOnly cookies; login attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll notify affected users if we ever become aware of a data breach affecting their information.

8. Children

Landroll Leader is a professional research tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect data from, children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes materially.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: help@landrollleader.com
